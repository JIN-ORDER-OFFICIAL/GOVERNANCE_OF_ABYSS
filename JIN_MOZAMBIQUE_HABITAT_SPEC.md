## 📌 Executive Summary
Designed specifically for coastal, high-humidity, and cyclone-prone emergency contexts across Northern and Central Mozambique (Cabo Delgado, Nampula, and Zambezia provinces). This specification adapts the decentralized engineering matrix established in the Chad Oasis Framework (Application ID: 95525) to withstand intense cyclonic wind loads, mitigate flash flooding, and guarantee clean water/shelter sovereignty without centralized supply chains.

## 🌴 Module 4.4: Tropical Cyclone & High-Precipitation Adaptation (JIN-IFP-TC)

### 1. Structural Engineering & Local Materials
- **Hydraulically Stabilized Earth Blocks (CSEB):** On-site production utilizing manual, unpowered mechanical block presses. Soil mixes stabilized with localized lime/natural binders and hydrophobic washes to withstand severe horizontal wind-driven precipitation without structural degradation.
- **Aerodynamic Double-Pitch Roof Truss:** Optimized low-wind-drag roof profiles anchored to deep sub-soil footings with high-tensile regional timber/bamboo rafters, mitigating cyclonic uplift forces while promoting rapid stormwater shedding.

### 2. Hydrological Protection & Public Health (WASH-Shelter Nexus)
- **Decentralized Roof-Catchment RWH:** Continuous food-grade perimeter guttering channeling rainwater into gravity-fed, non-electric bio-sand filtration cisterns (minimum 1,500L storage capacity per household unit), guaranteeing emergency drinking water during post-storm infrastructure collapses.
- **Surface Drainage & Sub-surface Soakaway Bioswales:** Elimination of stagnant standing pools through graded gravel-packed soakaway infiltration trenches positioned at each 5–10 unit cluster perimeter, directly suppressing malaria and cholera reproduction vectors.

### 3. Micro-Cluster Protective Layout & Community Safety
- **Radial Pod Structure:** Transitional shelters organized into self-governing clusters of 5 to 10 households facing an inner secure courtyard.
- **Integrated Passive Safety:** Standalone 12V DC micro-solar pods provide continuous perimeter illumination, eliminating unlit corridors to minimize Gender-Based Violence (GBV) risks and reinforce community self-policing.

## 🛠️ Implementation & Livelihood Guilds
- **Cash-for-Work Masonry Units:** Displaced persons and host community members form local construction guilds, circulating capital within the local economy while transferring permanent, deployable masonry and hydrological skills.
- **Zero Supply-Chain Dependency:** Eliminates recurring expenditures on imported short-life plastic tarpaulins, anchoring community resilience directly to localized natural assets.
